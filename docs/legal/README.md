# Legal documents

Enable Banking production-app activation requires the operator to provide
their own privacy policy and terms of service. Those documents are
operator-specific (your legal entity, your contact details, your
jurisdiction) and are intentionally not shipped in this repository.

See `docs/SPEC.md` §9.5 (Enable Banking production app activation) for
the activation flow. The EB application form lets you supply URLs to
your own legal documents — host them anywhere publicly reachable (e.g.
GitHub Pages, your own static site).
